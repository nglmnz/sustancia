Sustancia for universities

Potential applications of adaptive statistical learning in higher education and educational research.
Academic partnerships · proposed applications

A tool for learning, teaching, and research

Sustancia is designed to complement university statistics education. The first curricular domain is introductory statistics, with potential applications in social sciences, health sciences, and engineering.

Curriculum alignment

The model follows explicit learning outcomes and course content rather than an arbitrary AI-generated sequence. Its initial design draws on a 2022 introductory statistics syllabus covering foundational concepts, categorical and quantitative data, the normal distribution, and introductory inference. Each institutional implementation would require review of its own curriculum.

Three complementary perspectives

STUDENT

Understanding one’s learning

Explore strengths, difficulties, and progress across statistical concepts and operations without being reduced to a single grade.

INSTRUCTOR

Supporting learning

Identify recurring misconceptions and areas requiring reinforcement, follow class-level progress, and review proposed activities.

INSTITUTION

Continuous improvement

Study aggregated indicators by cohort, course, or program to investigate learning paths and direct educational support.

What could Sustancia contribute?

  • Initial diagnostic: an evidence-based approximation of each student’s mathematical and statistical starting point.
  • Situated practice: exercises drawing on data and problems relevant to different disciplines.
  • Recursive learning: first response, feedback, revision, and new evidence.
  • Curricular traceability: activities linked to concepts, operations, and reviewed sources.
  • Educational research: potential longitudinal analyses of students’ statistical reasoning.

These are design goals, not features already deployed or impact results already demonstrated.

Oversight and data access

Access should reflect responsibilities: students to their own profiles, instructors to course-relevant information, and institutions to aggregated indicators. Permissions, lawful basis, data minimization, retention, and safeguards must be defined before institutional deployment.

AI may support content discovery and review, but human educators remain responsible for curricular decisions and final approval of activities.

Development and collaboration

Sustancia is under development and research. The aim is to progress from the existing prototype to a pilot that can evaluate technical feasibility, pedagogical suitability, and ultimately evidence of learning.
